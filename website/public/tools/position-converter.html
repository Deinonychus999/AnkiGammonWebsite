About This Tool

            

                

                    
Paste or Upload

                    
Paste an XGID, GNUID, or OGID, or drop in a single-position .xgp file from eXtreme Gammon.

                

                

                    
Three Formats

                    
XGID (eXtreme Gammon), GNUID (GNU Backgammon), and OGID (OpenGammon) are all supported with automatic format detection.

                

                

                    
Export Your Board

                    
Choose from seven color schemes, change the board direction, then download or copy a high-resolution PNG.
